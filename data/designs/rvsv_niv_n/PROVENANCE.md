# rVSV Nipah nucleoprotein

Nipah N is an extra gene of class `henipavirus-nucleoprotein` after the filovirus glycoprotein of `rvsv_dg_filovirus_gp`. A 2014 Vaccine paper (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2014.02.087) clones NiV genes downstream of ZEBOV-GP. "rescued viruses are referred to as rVSV-ZEBOV-GP-NiVF, rVSV-ZEBOV-GP-NiVG and rVSV-ZEBOV-GP-NiVN." The same paper's F and G viruses are `rvsv_niv_f` and `rvsv_niv_g`.

Syrian hamsters (Harlan, Indianapolis, IN) were vaccinated intraperitoneally (i.p.) with 105 plaque forming units (PFU) of the specified rVSV vectors. "animals were challenged" i.p. with 1000 LD50 (6.8 × 104 TCID50) of NiV." Vaccinated animals with neutralizing antibody responses were completely protected from lethal NiV disease, whereas animals vaccinated with rVSV expressing NiV N showed only partial protection. The plan has two non-viral modules, so it is not compared with single-insert plans. No nucleoprotein sequence is stored.
