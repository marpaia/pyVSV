# rVSV HIV-1 Gag

VSV G remains. HIV-1 Gag, class `hiv-gag`, is inserted between G and L. The CT1 and CT9 truncations of G are different genomes and are not this record.

Clarke and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.01911-06) name the vector rVSVIN gag5 and state that the HIV-1 Gag gene is inserted between the G and L genes. In mice, intracranial inoculation of rVSV Indiana with or without that Gag insert was only marginally more attenuated than wild-type VSV, with an LD50 of about 2 to 5 PFU. No Gag sequence is stored.
