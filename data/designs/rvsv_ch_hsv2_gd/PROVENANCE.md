# rVSV Chandipura HSV-2 gD

Chandipura G replaces Indiana G in rVSVI-gD, and gD stays between that glycoprotein and L. The resultant plasmid vector pVSVI[GCh]-gD2 was used to rescue pseudotyped virus as described above. The Chandipura glycoprotein is encoded in the genome. Both genes are antigens, so this plan is not compared with single-insert plans.

BALB/c mice were primed either intranasally or intramuscularly with the indicated dose of rVSVCh-gD and boosted 3 to 4 weeks later by the same route with the corresponding rVSVI-gD. Survival after that prime and boost is not a protection observation on this genome or on `rvsv_gl_hsv2_gd`. No sequence is stored.
