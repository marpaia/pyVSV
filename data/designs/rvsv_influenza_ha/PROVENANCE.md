# rVSV influenza hemagglutinin

VSV glycoprotein remains a viral gene. Influenza hemagglutinin, class `influenza-hemagglutinin`, is an extra gene downstream of it. A 1997 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.71.8.5982-5989.1997) derived recombinant VSV expressing influenza hemagglutinin from a site downstream of the VSV glycoprotein gene. Neuraminidase in that paper was expressed from a different site and is not part of this architecture.

A 1998 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.72.6.4704-4711.1998) reports that this VSV-HA vector, given intranasally, raised neutralizing antibody and completely protected mice from bronchial pneumonia after a lethal dose of influenza A virus. The protection observation uses the lethal-challenge assay because that is the challenge result the library can record. The abstract says bronchial pneumonia, not death. No hemagglutinin sequence is stored.
