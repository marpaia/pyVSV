# rVSV IL-4

IL-4 is an extra gene of class `interleukin-4` between VSV G and L. Fernandez and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.76.2.895-904.2002) state: IL-4, TK, or GFP was inserted between the glycoprotein and polymerase protein genes of VSV. The recombinants showed no defects in replication compared to the wild-type virus. The sentence “Complete regression of a number of tumors was achieved” does not name IL-4 or TK, so it is not an observation on either plan. The GFP control is `rvsv_gfp`. No insert sequence is stored.
