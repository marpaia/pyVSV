# rVSV chikungunya envelope

Chikungunya E3-E2-6K-E1 is an extra gene of class `chikungunya-envelope` between VSV G and L. A 2013 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.01860-12) states: a synthetic gene encoding the CHIKV E3-E2-6K-E1 polyprotein was inserted between the G and L genes of VSV (Indiana) in the plasmid vector pVSVXN2 (23), and the resulting plasmid pVSV-CHIKV was used to recover VSV-CHIKV. VSV G is kept. The glycoprotein-deleted chimera built from this plasmid is `rvsv_chikv_e`.

Mouse neutralization and protection in that paper are recorded on the glycoprotein-deleted plan. The sentence that reports protection follows the statement that the chimeras propagate without VSV G complementation. No envelope sequence is stored.
