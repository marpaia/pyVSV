# single-cycle rVSV SARS-CoV spike

The glycoprotein module is a `sarbecovirus-spike` from SARS-CoV, not SARS-CoV-2. Replication is single-cycle. This is not `rvsv_dg_sars_cov_2_s`.

Kapadia and colleagues (Virology, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.virol.2008.03.002) state: "we constructed a replication-defective single-cycle VSV vector in which we replaced the VSV glycoprotein (G) gene with the SARS-CoV S gene." Mice were immunized intramuscularly. The neutralizing-antibody response was about ten-fold greater than the titer that paper treats as the threshold for protection from SARS-CoV infection, and greater than the response to a replication-competent VSV expressing the same spike by the same route. This paper does not report the challenge itself. No spike sequence is stored.
