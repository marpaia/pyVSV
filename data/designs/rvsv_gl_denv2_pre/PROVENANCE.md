# rVSV dengue-2 prM/E

Dengue-2 prM/E is an extra gene of class `dengue-envelope` between VSV G and L. A 2016 Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics paper (https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2016.1183857) states: inserted into the pVSV-XN2 plasmid, between the G and L genes of VSV genome to generate pVSV-DV2 prM/E. Infectious recombinant VSV-DENV-2 was recovered The insert is expressed together with VSV L, N, G, P and M proteins, so VSV G is kept.

Forty Balb/C mice were inoculated through subcutaneous (s.c.) route with VSV-DENV-2 vaccine in a two doses schedule 15 d apart. Mice were challenged with intracranial inoculation with 50 LD50 of neurovirulent DENV-2 TR1751. 80% of VSV-DENV-2 immunized mice survived the lethal challenge. No prM/E sequence is stored.
