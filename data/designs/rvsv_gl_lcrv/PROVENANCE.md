# rVSV LcrV after G

LcrV is an extra gene of class `yersinia-lcrv` between VSV G and L. The bacterial gene was expressed from the first position of the VSV genome (in front of the N gene) or the fifth position (between the G and the L genes) of the VSV recombinants [35], [53]. These viruses were designated VSV-LcrV1 and VSV-LcrV5. Recombinant VSVs expressing LcrV were recovered from these plasmids as described previously [34].

These vectors, given intranasally to mice, induced high antibody titers to the LcrV protein. Protection in the VSV-LcrV5-prime + boost group was also significant (p = 0.05), in which four out of nine mice survived challenge. That survival is the prime plus boost, so it is not protection by this genome alone. No LcrV sequence is stored.
