# rVSV Chandipura HIV gag

Chandipura G replaces Indiana G, and HIV-1 gag sits between that glycoprotein and L. Haglund and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.76.15.7506-7517.2002) state: The HIV-1 gag gene is expressed between the G and L genes, and the VSV Indiana serotype G (GI) was replaced with that of the Chandipura serotype (GCh). Infectious virus recombinants were recovered using published protocols. Gag was ligated into pVSV(GCh)-XN-1. Both genes are antigens, so this plan is not compared with single-insert plans.

Indiana VSV-Gag and VSV-Env in this paper are described as previous constructs, and this saved text does not state their positions. A tetramer result that primes with VSV-Gag and boosts with VSVCh-Gag is not recorded on this plan alone. No gag sequence is stored.
