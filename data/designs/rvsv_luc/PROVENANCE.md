# rVSV luciferase

Firefly luciferase is the first transcribed gene, between the leader and the nucleoprotein. VSV glycoprotein remains. A 2014 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.00019-14) constructed rVSV-Luc by placing the luciferase gene at the junction between the leader and the N gene, using plasmid pVSV1(+)LexxN. The same paper places norovirus capsid, and a luciferase control for a second insertion, at the junction between G and L. That double insertion is not this record. VSVΔG pseudotypes that carry luciferase only as a marker are not this record. No luciferase sequence is stored.
