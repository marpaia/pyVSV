# rVSV luciferase

Firefly luciferase is the first transcribed gene, between the leader and the nucleoprotein. VSV glycoprotein remains. Ma and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.00019-14) state: "rVSV-Luc contains the firefly luciferase (Luc) gene at the VSV gene junction between the leader sequence and the N gene." Plasmid pVSV1(+)LexxN carries the cloning sites at that junction. The same paper's mouse immunogenicity result is for rVSV-Luc-VP1, which also expresses norovirus capsid. That genome is not this record, so the result is not an observation here. VSVΔG pseudotypes that carry luciferase only as a marker are not this record. No luciferase sequence is stored.
