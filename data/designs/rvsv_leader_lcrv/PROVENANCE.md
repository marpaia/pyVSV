# rVSV LcrV

LcrV is an extra gene of class `yersinia-lcrv` in the first position. Palin and colleagues (Vaccine, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2006.08.010) state: The bacterial gene was expressed from the first position of the VSV genome (in front of the N gene) or the fifth position (between the G and the L genes) of the VSV recombinants [35], [53]. These viruses were designated VSV-LcrV1 and VSV-LcrV5. Recombinant VSVs expressing LcrV were recovered from these plasmids as described previously [34].

These vectors, given intranasally to mice, induced high antibody titers to the LcrV protein. Highly significant protection (p = 0.0002) was obtained in the VSV-LcrV1-prime + boost group where 9 out of 10 animals survived challenge. That survival is the prime plus boost, so it is not protection by this genome alone. No LcrV sequence is stored.
