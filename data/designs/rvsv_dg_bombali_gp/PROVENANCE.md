# rVSV Bombali ebolavirus glycoprotein

The glycoprotein module is a `bombali-ebolavirus-glycoprotein`. It replaces the VSV G open reading frame. The paper does not say whether the rescued virus completes a cycle without complementation, so replication is unknown.

Goldstein and colleagues (Nature Microbiology, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-018-0227-2) state: "Sequence encoding the full-length BOMV GP1,2 from the Angolan free-tailed bat was cloned between MluI and NotI restriction sites into the pVSV vector to replace the VSV G open reading frame. The resulting plasmid was used to rescue rVSV-BOMV GP." The rescued virus infected human 293FT cells and Vero cells. The paper does not report an animal challenge. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
