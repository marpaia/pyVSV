# rVSV Bundibugyo glycoprotein

Bundibugyo glycoprotein replaces VSV G. A 2013 PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases paper (https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pntd.0002600) describes recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) vectors expressing filovirus glycoproteins (GP), in place of the VSV glycoprotein. The Bundibugyo vector is rVSV-BEBOV-GP. Animals were vaccinated by intramuscular (i.m.) injection of an identical volume of PBS (Group 1), ∼2×107 plaque-forming units (PFU) of rVSV-BEBOV-GP (Group 2).

"all animals were challenged i.m. with 1,000 PFU of BEBOV." "Groups 2 and 4 were 100% protected against BEBOV." Group 2 is this genome. Group 4 is a heterologous Sudan then Zaire regimen and is not this genome. Cynomolgus macaques are the host. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
