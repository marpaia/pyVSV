# rVSV Bundibugyo glycoprotein

Bundibugyo glycoprotein replaces the VSV glycoprotein. The form is full length. The plan stores no strain. A 2013 PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases paper (https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pntd.0002600) describes recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) vectors expressing filovirus glycoproteins (GP), in place of the VSV glycoprotein. The methods state: "The rVSV-filovirus GP-vectors, rVSV-ZEBOV-GP (strain Mayinga), rVSV-SEBOV-GP (strain Boniface), and rVSV-BEBOV-GP (Fig. 1A) were recovered from cDNA." The same methods section states: "BEBOV, strain 200706291, was isolated from a fatal human case in western Uganda during the outbreak in 2007." That strain sentence names the challenge stock. The vaccine sentence does not give rVSV-BEBOV-GP a strain. Animals were vaccinated by intramuscular (i.m.) injection of an identical volume of PBS (Group 1), ∼2×107 plaque-forming units (PFU) of rVSV-BEBOV-GP (Group 2).

The host sentence is "Healthy, adult cynomolgus macaques (Macaca fascicularis)". "all animals were challenged i.m. with 1,000 PFU of BEBOV." The Group 2 result is "In this study the animals in Group 2 lacked clinical signs of disease, were below the limits of detection for viremia by plaque assay and qRT-PCR". Group 2 received ∼2×10^7 PFU intramuscularly, four weeks before challenge. Group 4 is a heterologous Sudan-then-Zaire prime-boost. This card records Group 2. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
