# rVSV GFP

VSV glycoprotein remains a viral gene. GFP is a reporter inserted between the glycoprotein and large polymerase genes. A 2016 Scientific Reports paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/srep24722) describes the Indiana virus it evolved as carrying a GFP insert between the G and L genes. The paper does not report a vaccine phenotype, so this record has no implementation and no observation. The G-deleted GFP backbone used for pseudotyping is a different architecture and is not this design. No GFP sequence is stored.
