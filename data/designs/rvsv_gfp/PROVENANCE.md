# rVSV GFP

VSV glycoprotein remains a viral gene. GFP is a reporter inserted between the glycoprotein and large polymerase genes. The full text of the 2016 Scientific Reports paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/srep24722, PMC4838867) states: "This mutant belongs to the Indiana VSV serotype and carries a GFP insert between the G and L genes." The experiment measures growth of an MΔ51 derivative of that virus. It does not report a vaccine phenotype for the GFP genome, so this record has no implementation and no observation. The G-deleted GFP backbone used for pseudotyping is a different architecture and is not this design. No GFP sequence is stored.
