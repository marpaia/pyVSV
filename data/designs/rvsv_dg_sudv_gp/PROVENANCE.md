# rVSV Sudan ebolavirus glycoprotein

The glycoprotein module is a `sudan-ebolavirus-glycoprotein`. It replaces the VSV glycoprotein. The strain is Boniface and the form is full length.

Geisbert and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.00561-09) state that a recombinant VSV "expresses a single filovirus glycoprotein (GP) in place of the VSV glycoprotein (G)." The same paper names the Sudan vector: "The recombinant VSVs expressing the glycoprotein (GP) of ZEBOV (strain Mayinga) (VSVΔG/ZEBOVGP), the GP of SEBOV (strain Boniface) (VSVΔG/SEBOVGP), or the GP of MARV (strain Musoke) (VSVΔG/MARVGP) were generated using the infectious clone for the VSV Indiana serotype, as described recently (11, 15)."

Geisbert and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.00456-08) gave that Sudan vector to rhesus macaques after exposure: "Here, we administered a VSV vector expressing the Sudan ebolavirus (SEBOV) glycoprotein to four rhesus macaques shortly after exposure to SEBOV." The result sentence is "All four animals survived SEBOV challenge, while a control animal that received a nonspecific vector developed fulminant SEBOV hemorrhagic fever and succumbed." The challenge is intramuscular: "Five healthy, filovirus-seronegative, rhesus macaques (4 to 7 kg) were challenged by intramuscular (i.m.) injection with 1,000 PFU of homologous SEBOV." The vaccine followed that challenge: "Approximately 20 to 30 min after SEBOV challenge, four of the animals received an i.m. injection at four different sites of the VSVΔG/SEBOVGP vector (∼2 × 10^7 PFU)." The observation stores host rhesus, timing post-exposure, vaccine route intramuscular, vaccine amount 2e7 PFU, and challenge amount 1000 PFU of SEBOV. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.

The 2009 paper also vaccinated macaques with a blend of the Sudan, Zaire, and Marburg vectors and then challenged some of them with Cote d'Ivoire ebolavirus. This card records the Sudan vector. That paper does not describe a Cote d'Ivoire glycoprotein vector.
