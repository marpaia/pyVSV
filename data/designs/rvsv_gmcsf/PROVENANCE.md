# rVSV GM-CSF

GM-CSF is an extra gene of class `gm-csf` in the first position, upstream of N. Ramsburg and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.79.24.15043-15053.2005) state: an rVSV (VSV-GMCSF1) expressing high levels of murine granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF) from the first position in the viral genome is highly attenuated in terms of viral dissemination and pathogenesis after intranasal delivery to mice. Recombinant viruses were recovered from these plasmids as described previously.

this highly attenuated virus generated antibody and T-cell responses equivalent to those induced by a control virus expressing enhanced green fluorescent protein (EGFP) from the first position (VSV-EGFP1). The antibody sentence does not name the route. Glycoprotein-deleted vectors in the same paper are not this genome. No cytokine sequence is stored.
