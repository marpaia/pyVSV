# rVSV SFTSV glycoprotein

The glycoprotein module is an antigen of class `bandavirus-glycoprotein`. A 2019 npj Vaccines paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41541-018-0096-y) replaces VSV G with SFTSV Gn/Gc. The human codon-optimized glycoprotein Gn/Gc ORF of the Chinese clade of SFTSV, AH12 strain (GenBank accession number ADZ04482.1)42 was cloned into a rVSVΔG vector as the vaccine candidate43 (designated as rVSV-SFTSV/AH12-GP). The paper calls it "a replication-competent rVSV vector expressing SFTSV Gn/Gc." MluI at nt3075 and NotI at nt4621 flank the G-protein-encoding region.

Immunocompetent mice: Six-to-eight-week-old C57/BL6 mice were i.p. immunized with a single dose of 2 × 104 PFU of rVSV-SFTSV/AH12-GP. Immunocompromised mice were "challenged with 2 × 104 FFU of SFTSV Wuhan strain." "rVSV-SFTSV could completely and durably protect Ifnar–/– mice from lethal challenge with SFTSV." The challenge sentence does not name the challenge route. Heartland virus Gn/Gc is the same glycoprotein replacement and is not drawn separately. An eGFP reporter virus in the same paper is not this plan. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
