# rVSV rabies glycoprotein

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `rabies-glycoprotein`. A 2025 Molecular Therapy paper (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2025.02.038) replaced the VSV Indiana glycoprotein coding sequence in p3.1-VSV with rabies ERA G. A luciferase version of the same genome adds a reporter transcription unit between that glycoprotein and L. The luciferase unit is not drawn on this architecture. Oral vaccination protected 100% of mice challenged intracranially with 100 LD50 of rabies virus CVS-24. Dogs developed neutralizing antibody. The host `other` on the dog observation is the library's category for a species it does not name separately. Pseudotyped particles used only to measure rabies antibodies are not this record. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
