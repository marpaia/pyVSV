# rVSV CAT

Chloramphenicol acetyltransferase is a reporter downstream of the glycoprotein gene. The saved text for Schnell and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 1996, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.70.4.2318-2323.1996) is the abstract. A new transcription unit was generated in the 3' noncoding region of the vesicular stomatitis virus (VSV) glycoprotein gene by introducing the smallest conserved sequence found at each VSV gene junction. Infectious recombinant VSV was recovered from this construct and expressed the enzyme. No animal result is in the saved text. No reporter sequence is stored.
