# rvsv_gl_niv_g

Chattopadhyay and Rose (Journal of Virology, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.01852-10) built VSV recombinants expressing Nipah virus G or F. The replication-competent pair keeps VSV G and carries the Nipah gene between G and L: "NiV genes were inserted into the plasmid vector pVSVXN2 (30) between the VSV G and L genes (Fig. 1 A)." The single-cycle pair deletes that VSV G gene. This record is VSV-G(NiV). The replication-defective vectors generated Nipah neutralizing antibody in mice. The paper does not state the immunization route. No Nipah sequence is stored.
