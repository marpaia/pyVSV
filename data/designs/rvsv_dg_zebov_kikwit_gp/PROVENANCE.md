# rVSV-ZEBOV Kikwit GP

The glycoprotein is Zaire ebolavirus strain Kikwit-1995, full length, and replaces VSV G. The product name on this genome is Ervebo.

Regules and colleagues (New England Journal of Medicine, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414216) state: "The rVSV-ZEBOV vaccine candidate is a live attenuated recombinant virus consisting of the VSV strain Indiana, with the glycoprotein of the ZEBOV Kikwit 1995 strain replacing the gene for the VSV envelope glycoprotein." The same paper states: "The most common adverse events were injection-site pain, fatigue, myalgia, and headache." That observation is prophylactic. The adverse-event sentence does not state one dose.

Agnandji and colleagues (New England Journal of Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1502924) state: "No serious vaccine-related adverse events were reported." The conclusions state: "In these studies, rVSV-ZEBOV was reactogenic but immunogenic after a single dose and warrants further evaluation for safety and efficacy."

Huttner and colleagues (Lancet Infectious Diseases, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1016/S1473-3099(15)00154-1) state: "Injections were given intramuscularly over 30 s into the deltoid." Both observations are prophylactic. The low-dose reactogenicity observation is its own row, with vaccine amount 3e5 PFU: "At 3 × 105 pfu, early-onset reactogenicity remained frequent (45 [88%] of 51 compared with 50 [98%] of 51 high dose and two [15%] of 13 placebo recipients), but mild." The antibody observation does not store a dose: "Although seropositivity rates remained similarly high (48 [94%] of 51), day-28 EBOV-glycoprotein-binding and neutralising antibody titres were lower in low-dose versus high-dose vaccinees."

Henao-Restrepo and colleagues (Lancet, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)32621-6) report the ring vaccination trial: "No cases of Ebola virus disease occurred 10 days or more after randomisation among randomly assigned contacts and contacts of contacts vaccinated in immediate clusters versus 16 cases (7 clusters affected) among all eligible individuals in delayed clusters." That result is the field-efficacy observation. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
