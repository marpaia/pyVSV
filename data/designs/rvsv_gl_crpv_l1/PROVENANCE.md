# rVSV CRPV L1

CRPV L1 is an extra gene of class `papillomavirus-capsid` between VSV G and L. Reuter and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.76.17.8900-8909.2002) state: The CRPV-L1 gene was inserted between the VSV G and L genes. VSV-L1 was recovered. The control VSV-GFP insert is `rvsv_gfp`, and the papilloma result is not attributed to it.

Rabbits were completely protected from CRPV-induced papillomas after VSV-L1 vaccination and boost given intranasally or intramuscularly. That result is stored as an unmodeled papilloma outcome. A protection filter does not return this plan. No L1 sequence is stored.
