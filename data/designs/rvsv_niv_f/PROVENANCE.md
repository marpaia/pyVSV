# rVSV Nipah fusion

de Wit and colleagues (eBioMedicine, January 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2022.104405) tested a VSV-EBOV vaccine expressing Nipah virus fusion protein F in African green monkeys. The parent design is `rvsv_dg_filovirus_gp`. The added module is class `henipavirus-fusion`.

The abstract does not state the genomic coordinate of the added Nipah gene. That module's locus is unknown, and no derivation is recorded. The backbone modules keep the parent design's loci. No fusion-protein sequence is stored.

The abstract states a lower humoral response than the glycoprotein candidate, complete homologous protection, and partial heterologous protection. Those observations belong to the preclinical implementation.
