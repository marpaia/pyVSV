# rVSV Nipah fusion

de Wit and colleagues (eBioMedicine, January 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2022.104405) tested a VSV-EBOV vaccine expressing Nipah virus fusion protein F in African green monkeys. The parent design is `rvsv_dg_filovirus_gp`. The added module is class `henipavirus-fusion`.

The NiV Bangladesh F open reading frame is cloned into the same site as the glycoprotein candidate: downstream of the Ebola Kikwit glycoprotein in pVSVXN2ΔG-EBOVKik GP. The fusion gene sits after the filovirus glycoprotein and before L. No fusion-protein sequence is stored.

Vaccination was intramuscular, 10^7 PFU. Challenge was a lethal dose of NiV Bangladesh or NiV Malaysia, 10^5 TCID50 intranasally and 10^5 TCID50 intratracheally. Every animal survived the homologous challenge. Three of four survived the heterologous challenge. The neutralization and protection notes belong to the preclinical implementation.

A 2014 Vaccine paper (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2014.02.087) placed Nipah F or G downstream of ZEBOV-GP on this same arrangement. Syrian hamsters (Harlan, Indianapolis, IN) were vaccinated intraperitoneally (i.p.) with 105 plaque forming units (PFU) of the specified rVSV vectors. "animals were challenged" i.p. with 1000 LD50 (6.8 × 104 TCID50) of NiV." Vaccinated animals with neutralizing antibody responses were completely protected from lethal NiV disease, whereas animals vaccinated with rVSV expressing NiV N showed only partial protection. Nipah N on that backbone is `rvsv_niv_n`. No Nipah sequence is stored.
