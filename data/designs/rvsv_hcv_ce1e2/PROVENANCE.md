# rVSV HCV core E1 E2

HCV core, E1, and E2 are one extra antigen of class `hepacivirus-structural`. The locus is unknown: Ezelle and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.76.23.12325-12334.2002) clone them into the XhoI and NheI sites of pVSV-XN2, and the saved text does not say between G and L. The Core, E1, and E2 regions (aa 1 to 746) of the HCV polypeptide (NIHJ1 provided by T. Miyamura) were cloned into the XhoI and NheI sites of the rVSV replicon vector pVSV-XN2. "the VSV G and M proteins were expressed," so VSV G is kept. The virus grew to high titers in vitro and efficiently expressed the incorporated HCV gene product, which became fully processed into the individual HCV structural proteins.

BALB/c mice (age, 6 to 8 weeks; Jackson Laboratories) were intravenously (i.v.) injected with 2.5 × 106 PFU of VSV-GFP or VSV-HCV-C/E1/E2. Mice immunized with VSV-HCV-C/E1/E2 generated cell-mediated immune responses to all of the HCV structural proteins. This plan is not compared with positioned plans. No HCV sequence is stored.
