# rVSV Lassa glycoprotein complex

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `arenavirus-gpc`. Garbutt and colleagues (Journal of Virology, May 2004, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.78.10.5458-5465.2004) inserted Lassa virus GPC between the matrix and polymerase genes of a glycoprotein-deleted, replication-competent rVSV. That construct is the preclinical implementation. The mouse protection result in that paper is the Zaire Ebola glycoprotein vector, so this design does not carry it.

Human observations belong to the clinical implementation from Malkin and colleagues, New England Journal of Medicine, 6 November 2025, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2501073. That trial studied replication-competent rVSVΔG-LASV-GPC in healthy adults. Adults in the United States and Liberia received the vaccine or placebo intramuscularly, as a single dose of 2×10^4 to 2×10^7 PFU or two doses of 2×10^7 PFU. No serious vaccine-related adverse events and no hearing loss were reported. Every dose induced binding and neutralizing antibody. Early systemic reactogenicity was dose-dependent and transient. No GPC sequence is stored.

A 2019 npj Vaccines paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41541-019-0104-x) vaccinated outbred guinea pigs with a single intraperitoneal dose: "1 × 106 PFU of VSVΔG-LASVGPC by the intraperitoneal route". 100% protection at days 7 and 14 post-vaccination. The challenge virus is GPA-LASV. The immunization dose is not stored as the challenge dose. No GPC sequence is stored.
