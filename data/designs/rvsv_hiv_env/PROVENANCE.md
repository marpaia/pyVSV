# rVSV HIV envelope

VSV glycoprotein remains a viral gene. An additional antigen of class `hiv-envelope` is present and its locus is unknown. A 1997 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.71.7.5060-5068.1997) generated replication-competent recombinant VSV expressing the HIV-1 envelope protein, or an HIV-VSV chimeric envelope, from extra transcription units. The abstract does not state where the extra gene sits. No envelope sequence is stored.
