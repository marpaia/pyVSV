# rVSV HIV envelope

VSV glycoprotein remains a viral gene. HIV envelope, class `hiv-envelope`, is an extra transcription unit between G and L. Johnson, Schnell, Buonocore, and Rose (Journal of Virology, 1997, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.71.7.5060-5068.1997) cloned the envelope coding sequence into pVSV-XN1: "The PCR product was cloned through the XhoI and XbaI sites into pVSV-XN1 (26) that had been digested with XhoI and NheI." The same paper states the meaning of that site: "The insertion of an extra transcription unit between the VSV G and L genes was found to support high-level, stable expression of an extra mRNA and protein (26)." The viruses are replication-competent. The paper reports expression and incorporation into particles, not a vaccine challenge, so this record has no observation. No envelope sequence is stored.
