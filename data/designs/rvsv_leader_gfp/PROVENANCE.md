# rVSV leader GFP

GFP is a reporter in the first position, upstream of N. The same paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.79.24.15043-15053.2005) describes this highly attenuated virus generated antibody and T-cell responses equivalent to those induced by a control virus expressing enhanced green fluorescent protein (EGFP) from the first position (VSV-EGFP1). Recombinant viruses were recovered from these plasmids as described previously. The attenuation described for VSV-GMCSF1 is not recorded on this genome. No GFP sequence is stored.
