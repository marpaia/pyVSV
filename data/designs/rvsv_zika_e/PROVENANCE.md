# rVSV Zika envelope

VSV glycoprotein remains. The Zika envelope gene, class `zika-envelope`, sits between the glycoprotein and large polymerase genes. A 2018 Nature Communications paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-05276-4) cloned anchor-C–prM–E–NS1, and the envelope constructs, into pVSV(+)GxxL at the SmaI and XhoI sites between G and L. A single dose of the attenuated prM-E-NS1 virus protected mice from Zika challenge. A 2017 Journal of Immunology paper (https://doi.org/10.4049/jimmunol.1602180) describes wild-type and matrix-mutant rVSV expressing Zika envelope. Its full text was not available, so that implementation does not establish the insertion site. No envelope sequence is stored.
