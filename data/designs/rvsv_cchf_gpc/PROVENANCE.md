# rVSV Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever glycoprotein

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `nairovirus-glycoprotein`. A 2019 Scientific Reports paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44210-6) cloned codon-optimized CCHFV glycoprotein precursor into pVSVΔG after excising a previously inserted Ebola glycoprotein at the MluI and NheI sites. The first recovery, complemented with VSV G in trans, was single-cycle. Passage without complementation produced the replication-competent vaccine vector ΔGrVSV-CCHFV-GPCΔ. That vector protected STAT1 knockout mice from challenge. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
