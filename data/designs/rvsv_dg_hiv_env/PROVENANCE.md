# Single-cycle rVSV HIV envelope

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `hiv-envelope`. Replication is single-cycle. A 2005 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.79.21.13231-13238.2005) generated a G-deleted VSV recombinant expressing a secreted form of HIV envelope, so expression of envelope would not let the vector replicate in CD4-positive cells. The abstract compares cellular immune responses with replication-competent VSV vectors and does not name the host species for that comparison, so this record has no observation. No envelope sequence is stored.
