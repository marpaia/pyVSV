# Single-cycle rVSV HIV envelope

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `hiv-envelope`. Replication is single-cycle. Publicover, Ramsburg, and Rose (Journal of Virology, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.79.21.13231-13238.2005) state: "Because expression of HIV Env by G-deleted VSV might allow replication in human cells expressing CD4, we generated a single-cycle VSV recombinant expressing a secreted form of the HIV Env protein." In mice, by the intramuscular route, that vector matched a replication-competent VSV vector for primary and memory CD8 T-cell responses and for antibody responses to Env. No envelope sequence is stored.
