# rVSV RSV fusion

VSV glycoprotein remains. RSV fusion, class `pneumovirus-fusion`, sits between the glycoprotein and large polymerase genes. A 2001 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.75.22.11079-11087.2001) states that the original full-length genomes contained RSV F between the VSV G and L genes, and RSV attachment between the VSV M and G genes. Replication-competent VSV expressing RSV F induced neutralizing antibodies and completely protected mice from RSV challenge. The same paper deletes VSV G so that the RSV glycoprotein gene occupies the G position. That glycoprotein-deleted virus is not this record. A 1999 Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1006/viro.1998.9535) is the earlier description of these additional genes. Its full text was not available. No fusion sequence is stored.
