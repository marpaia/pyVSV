# rVSV HCV E1/E2

VSV G is replaced by HCV E1 and E2 glycoproteins, class `hepacivirus-glycoprotein`. Buonocore and colleagues (Journal of Virology, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.76.14.6865-6872.2002) state: "the gene encoding the VSV G protein was deleted and replaced by one or both of the E1G and E2G genes, together with a green fluorescent protein gene." The phrase covers one or both of E1G and E2G. "these virions did not infect cell lines that have been reported to support at least a low level of HCV infection and replication." The particles are single-cycle. GFP is in the construction sentence and is not drawn, because its order relative to E1 and E2 is unstated.

G-kept E1G and E2G clones in the same paper are ligated into pVSV-XN2. That file does not say the site is between G and L, so those clones are not an insert-after-G plan. No glycoprotein sequence is stored.
