# rVSV West Nile envelope

West Nile envelope is an extra antigen of class `west-nile-envelope`. Its locus is unknown. A 2009 Vaccine paper (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2008.11.087) states: We constructed recombinant VSVs specifying either the Indiana or Chandipura virus G glycoprotein and expressing the West Nile virus (WNV) envelope (E) glycoprotein. The saved text does not say between G and L. Recombinant VSV was recovered after co-transfection of pVSV-XN2-E. That recovery sentence names the Indiana plasmid. VSV G is kept on this plan.

Mice were intranasally vaccinated using a prime (Indiana)-boost (Chandipura) immunization approach and challenged with the virulent WNV-LSU-AR01. Ninety-percent (9 of 10) of the vaccinated mice survived as compared to 10% of the mock-vaccinated mice after WNV lethal challenge. That survival is the prime-boost regimen, so it is not a protection observation on this plan. No envelope sequence is stored.
