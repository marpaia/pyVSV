# rVSV chikungunya envelope

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `chikungunya-envelope`. A 2013 Journal of Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1128/jvi.01860-12) built a chimeric VSV expressing the chikungunya envelope polyprotein E3-E2-6K-E1 in place of VSV G. The methods construct that vaccine by deleting VSV G from a genome that first carried the polyprotein between G and L. The G-retaining virus, VSV-CHIKV, is recorded as `rvsv_gl_chikv_e`. The chimera propagates without VSV G complementation. Mice received the vectors intramuscularly. One dose induced neutralizing antibodies and protected mice from chikungunya infection. A La Crosse virus pseudotype that the corpus search grouped with chikungunya is not this record. No envelope sequence is stored.
