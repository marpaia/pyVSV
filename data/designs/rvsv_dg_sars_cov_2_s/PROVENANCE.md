# rVSV SARS-CoV-2 spike

The VSV glycoprotein part is an antigen of class `sarbecovirus-spike`. A 2020 Nature Communications paper (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20228-7) describes a replication-competent recombinant VSV-ΔG-spike vaccine in which the glycoprotein of VSV is replaced by the spike protein of SARS-CoV-2. A single dose induced neutralizing antibodies in golden Syrian hamsters. That paper is the preclinical implementation.

A 2022 Vaccines paper (https://doi.org/10.3390/vaccines10020291) describes BriLife (rVSV-ΔG-spike), the same replacement, then in phase II trials. Human sera from vaccinees neutralized SARS-CoV-2 variants. That paper is the clinical implementation. No spike sequence is stored.
