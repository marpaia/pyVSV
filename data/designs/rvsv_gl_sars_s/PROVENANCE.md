# rVSV SARS-CoV spike

SARS-CoV spike is an extra gene of class `sarbecovirus-spike` between VSV G and L. A 2005 Virology paper (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.virol.2005.06.016) states: the SARS-CoV S coding sequence inserted between the VSV G and VSV L genes. We recovered a recombinant VSV (designated VSV-S). VSV-S-infected cells expressed the five VSV proteins, so VSV G is kept.

mice immunized with VSV-S or SARS-CoV were completely protected and controlled the SARS-CoV challenge as indicated by SARS-CoV titers at or below the detection limits in both the lungs and nasal turbinates. The saved text does not name the immunization route or a challenge dose, and it does not contain the word lethal. The assay on the observation is the library's challenge assay. This genome is distinct from the single-cycle SARS-CoV replacement `rvsv_dg_sars_s`. No spike sequence is stored.
