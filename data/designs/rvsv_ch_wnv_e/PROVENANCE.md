# rVSV Chandipura West Nile envelope

Chandipura G is the glycoprotein and West Nile envelope is a second antigen whose locus is unknown. We constructed recombinant VSVs specifying either the Indiana or Chandipura virus G glycoprotein and expressing the West Nile virus (WNV) envelope (E) glycoprotein. The recovery sentence names pVSV-XN2-E, the Indiana plasmid, so replication of this Chandipura genome is unknown. The plan has two non-viral modules and an unknown locus, so it is not compared with single-insert plans.

Mice were intranasally vaccinated using a prime (Indiana)-boost (Chandipura) immunization approach and challenged with the virulent WNV-LSU-AR01. Ninety-percent (9 of 10) of the vaccinated mice survived as compared to 10% of the mock-vaccinated mice after WNV lethal challenge. That result is not a protection observation on this genome. No envelope sequence is stored.
